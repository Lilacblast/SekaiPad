# Community guidelines

SekaiPad is an experimental open-source input tool. We welcome hardware
reports, bug reports and contributions from people with varied experience.

Please be respectful, discuss technical claims with evidence, and avoid
harassment, personal attacks or needless disclosure of other people's
information. Keep issue reproductions small and privacy-preserving; never
post credentials, private HID captures, game login screens, system dumps or
personal device identifiers.

Maintainers may remove material that violates these guidelines or the
repository's safety policy. If the matter is security-sensitive, follow
`SECURITY.md` instead of posting a public issue.
